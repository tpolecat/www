---
title: Microscope
---

A microscope is essential for working with SMD parts and for inspecting things up close. I use mine all the time. This is a large purchase and it took me a while to figure out what I wanted, and I'm very happy with the result.

My main goal was to make it effortless to swing the scope over and start using it in less than five seconds. This means integrated lighting and cable management so I don't need to move anything out of the way.
