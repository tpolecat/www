## Assortments

I'm a big fan of parts assortments from Amazon. My favorite supplier is a company called [EEEEE](https://www.amazon.com/stores/EEEEE/page/0DDAEA46-863D-4737-92DE-C8CDFAB4A93E?ref_=ast_bln) but I have also had good luck with [Bojack]. I use the following assortments **all the time**. 

- [1/4 Watt Resistors](https://www.amazon.com/EEEEE-Resistor-Compliant-resistors-Assortment/dp/B08BNDL6DS?ref_=ast_sto_dp). I really like this assortment because I can flip through the book to find the values I need. The values are accurate, but the legs are very thin and I find it hard to read the color bands on metal film resistors because of the blue background. So as I use these up I'm replacing them with carbon film resistors.
- [Potentiometers](https://www.amazon.com/EEEEE-Potentiometer-Assortment-Individual-Compartment/dp/B09HJTGB7R?ref_=ast_sto_dp&th=1). I end up using these pretty often, but they're not super breadboard friendly.
- [Trimmers]
- [Ceramic capacitors](https://www.amazon.com/EEEEE-Multilayer-Monolithic-assortment-Individual/dp/B09H2N3L4B?ref_=ast_sto_dp&th=1). Great assortment. Most breadboard projects call for capacitors in this range.
- [Film Capacitors]
- [Electrolytic Capacitors](https://www.amazon.com/BOJACK-Electrolytic-Capacitor-Assortment-0-1uF－1000uF/dp/B07PBQXQNQ/ref=sr_1_3?crid=25Q5U42T8KB8L&keywords=electrolytic+capacitor+kit&qid=1707757239&s=industrial&sprefix=electrolyic+capacitor+kit%2Cindustrial%2C101&sr=1-3).
- [Tactile Switches](https://www.amazon.com/TWTADE-Yellow-Orange-6x6x5mm-Tactile/dp/B07C7211PJ/ref=sr_1_51?crid=23HHTF2WUYJVB&keywords=tactile+switch+assortment&qid=1707758236&s=industrial&sprefix=tactile+switch+assortment%2Cindustrial%2C104&sr=1-51). The box is cute and I really like having a bunch of colors.
- LEDs. I like 3mm LEDs for breadboarding.
- [BJT Transistors](https://www.amazon.com/EEEEE-Transistors-Transistor-Assortment-Compartment/dp/B095NMT2SL?ref_=ast_sto_dp&th=1) and [Mosfets](https://www.amazon.com/EEEEE-Transistor-Assortment-Channel-RFP30N06LE/dp/B09K3T5LSN?ref_=ast_sto_dp). It took a while for me to learn how to use these parts I'm using them more and more now.
- [Diodes](https://www.amazon.com/EEEEE-Assortment-Rectifier-Recovery-Switching/dp/B08SBTS2KZ?ref_=ast_sto_dp) and Zener Diodes. I use these less often than I expected, but they still come up from time to time.
- [Inductors](https://www.amazon.com/EEEEE-Assortment-Resistance-Individual-Compartment/dp/B09HKNT59M?ref_=ast_sto_dp). I have only used these for experiments so far.


## Integrated Circuits


[ACR story]

For new parts I prefer to order from Mouser because their cheapest shipping gets here overnight, and their site is much less overwhelming than DigiKey (where I will go if I need a larger selection). For obsolete parts I have had good luck with Unicorn Electronics and North American suppliers on eBay. I have gotten a few batches of fake parts from Chinese suppliers on eBay and AliExpress, so I avoid them now.
